## New Finding — the red-evidence gate's universe is declared, not derived

`redEvidenceDirs` (`internal/store/redevidence_harness_test.go:86`) is a hand-authored
`map[dir]map[patch]testName`. Its behaviour splits at two levels:

- **Within a listed directory:** fails closed. Set-equality runs both ways (glob vs. map), and
  a zero-patch glob for a listed directory is a `t.Fatalf`. A stale mapping entry fails too.
- **Across directories:** fails open. A phase directory absent from the map is simply not
  covered, and nothing reports its absence.

Phases 04 and 05 are absent from the map. For phase 04 that is consistent with the accepted
residual — but the acceptance is recorded in planning docs, not in the harness, so the code
carries no trace that a directory is *deliberately* uncovered versus *forgotten*. A future
phase adding red-evidence must remember to add its directory; nothing will remind it.

This is the same family as the milestone's other vacuous-gate findings, one level up: the gate
is sound, and its *input set* is the maintained thing.
