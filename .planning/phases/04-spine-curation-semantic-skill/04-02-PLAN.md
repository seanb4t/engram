Administer the tracer's end-to-end proof. Build a genuinely adversarial fixture — an `overlapping`
pair engineered to read as `same-fact` — and run it past a zero-context subagent to establish
whether the consent gate in `curating-spine/SKILL.md` holds when the reader's own judgment is

proved the mirror-image property (does an agent *notice and propose*, a false-negative risk). This
proves the runaway risk: given a proposal the reader finds obviously right and which is actually
wrong, does it still stop and ask.

## Why this plan is its own wave, and who may administer it

Tracer-first: plan 04-01's slice is not proven until this read runs. Plan 04-03's expansion is
gated behind it deliberately — if the gate does not hold, the consent section is redesigned before
three more sections are written on top of it.

**D-14 (v0.12.x Phase 6) governs administration and resolves an apparent contradiction.** 04-CONTEXT.md
paraphrases D-14 as "neither executor nor orchestrator can administer the test", but the source text
(`06-CONTEXT.md:144-149`) says something narrower and 06-COLD-READ.md's own header records
"Administered: by the orchestrator per D-14". Verbatim: *"the cold read is performed by a fresh
subagent, not by a blocking human checkpoint... which the orchestrator cannot self-administer,
having produced the context."* The constraint is on **self**-administration and on the *subagent's*
context, not on who spawns it. So: this is `type="auto"`, not a checkpoint. The executor of this
plan did not author `SKILL.md` (plan 04-01 did, in a separate context), and the subagent receives
zero phase context regardless. Both conditions hold.

## Flagged Assumptions (no-silent-drop ledger)

Continuing plan 04-01's ledger — the 2nd of the 5 probe-surfaced items lands here.

1. **REQ-consent-adversarial-proof / `unclassified` — stays `unresolved`.** An unclassified probe row
   is never auto-resolved with a backstop and never dismissed. The assumption: that a single
   fixture, single subagent, single model run is sufficient evidence. RESEARCH.md flags this as
   assumption A3 and 06-COLD-READ.md's own Limits section states the precedent's position plainly —
   *"One subagent, one scenario, one model... A pass here is evidence the shape works, not proof it
   works for every candidate."* Carried forward unverified, deliberately matching the precedent's
   scope rather than silently expanding it.
2. **The read is administered against the tracer-stage file, not the shipped file.** Plan 04-03 adds
   sections around the consent path. Mitigation, not elimination: 04-03 re-runs plan 04-01's Gates A
   and B, which pin the consent step and verb table byte-for-byte, so the tested text provably does
   not drift. The residual risk — that added surrounding content dilutes the instruction — is real
   and must be named in the Limits section rather than papered over.
3. **RESEARCH.md assumption A2** (the identity axis is a stronger adversarial fixture than the
   staleness axis) is adopted, not proven. D-08 names `overlapping` as "the dangerous middle" in the
   user's own words, which is the evidence for it. A reviewer preferring a staleness-axis fixture
   should say so; do not treat A2 as settled.
