<objective>
Wire the verifier chain into the ONE call site that changes — `withAuth` at `cmd/engram/serve.go`
(SC1 / D-01 / D-03) — building each of the three verifiers conditionally on its own config presence,
wrapping them with `chainVerifier`, and passing the single result to `mcpauth.RequireBearerToken`.
Prove, with a `TestWriteParity`-analogous integration test, that owner-claim resolution and isolation
are identical regardless of which verifier in the chain answered (D-07). Finally, deliver the
"written" half of D-13/D-15: document the service-auth config surface, the no-revocation kill-switch,
and the global cross-tenant shared-read decision (an ADR + config guide).

This is the Wave-2 integration plan: it consumes the service verifier (Plan 01), static-token
verifier (Plan 02), chain combinator (Plan 03), and `ServiceAuthConfig` (Plan 04).

Purpose: turn the composed pieces into the live request path with human-only behavior preserved, and
close SC5's "written" requirement.
Output: `withAuth` chain wiring, the chain parity test, config-guide + auth-reference docs, and the
cross-tenant shared-read ADR.
</objective>
