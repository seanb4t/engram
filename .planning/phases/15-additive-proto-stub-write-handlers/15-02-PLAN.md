<objective>
Add the idempotency-level ban as a build gate: fail any build (local `task proto:lint`
and the CI `buf` job) if a mutating RPC is ever annotated side-effect-free, because that
annotation makes the RPC GET-reachable and CSRF-exploitable (PITFALLS.md Pitfall 2,
verified against connect-go's `protocol_connect.go`).

Purpose: Enforce Success Criterion 2 as a permanent, cheap grep gate that runs both
locally (dev parity, D-09) and in CI (D-09 + this repo's bare-runner CI convention).
Output: A new `proto:lint` command in Taskfile plus a mirrored inline step in the CI
`buf` job.
</objective>
