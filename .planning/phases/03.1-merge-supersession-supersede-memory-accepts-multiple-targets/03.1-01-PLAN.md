**Checkpoint:** assumption-delta — FIRED (`detected: true`, kind `pluralization`, term "multiple").

**Primary noun:** the supersession *target set* (`supersedes`).

**Decision:** `promote` — not `add-alongside`. `supersedes` becomes a list; the single-target call
becomes the one-element case of the general form. The wire shape is ALWAYS an array (D-03). The
decoder is tolerant (accepts a bare string or a list) with NO backfill command (D-04), because a
legacy scalar record loses nothing functionally under a tolerant read.

**Rationale:** `add-alongside` — keeping `supersedes` scalar and introducing a second plural key —
would leave the singular form required and silently contradict the generalized intent: two payload
keys encoding overlapping truth, and every future reader forced to know which one wins. Locked by
CONTEXT.md D-01/D-03/D-04 and confirmed by the developer.

**Not pluralized:** `superseded_by` stays scalar and is untouched (D-02). Each predecessor has
exactly one successor; the four `IsEmpty("superseded_by")` recall gates (`store.go:954`, `1054`,
`1191`, `1426`) are not modified by this phase. The pluralization is strictly one-directional.

**Companion invariant test (required by the checkpoint):** `TestSupersedeMultiStamp` uses N=3, not
N=1, precisely so that a future phase quietly reintroducing the singular assumption goes red rather
than passing on a degenerate case.
