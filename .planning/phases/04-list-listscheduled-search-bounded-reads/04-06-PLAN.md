<objective>
Wire the two boundary contracts the store now supports: let a caller's full-vs-summary choice reach
the fetch instead of being applied only after a full payload was already read, and publish the
documented maximum as a named rejection on every recall count knob. This is the plan where decision
B becomes visible to callers.

Decisions covered: D-02 (one maximum across every list `limit` and search `k` surface), D-03 (the
rule listing inherits the same ceiling and says so), D-08 (a zero count keeps each surface's existing
default), D-10 (rejection with the named hint, classified as malformed, on every surface).

Assumption-delta on record: the noun this phase settles is **the recall count maximum** — one
identifier serving list and search alike — and the decision is **promote**: the numeric maximum
becomes the primary contract on every surface and the implicit unbounded meaning is retired, not kept
alongside it. Rationale: 04-CONTEXT D-01 and D-02, chosen by the user on 2026-09-19. The invariant
that keeps it true is this plan's seven-surface table.

Purpose: REQ-list-limit-contract-decided, plus the surface half of REQ-list-bounded,
REQ-list-scheduled-bounded and REQ-search-k-bounded.

Output: a projection-carrying typed core, a rejection on seven surfaces, and the rule listing's
restated contract.
</objective>
