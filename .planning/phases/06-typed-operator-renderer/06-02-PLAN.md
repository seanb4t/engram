<objective>
Publish the text lane's stability status: `--output text` is a human-readable view, not a stable
interface; `--output json` is the contract.

Purpose: 06-CONTEXT.md D-03 is what makes the text lane safe to evolve forever and is the mechanism
by which byte-identical-sentence guarantees dissolve rather than being traded away. It is a one-way
decision — once published as unstable and evolved, the prior text output cannot be restored as a
contract — so it carries a decision checkpoint.

Output: the operator `--output` flag help, the regenerated help golden, and the docs-site CLI guide.
</objective>

  <name>Task 1: Confirm publishing `--output text` as an explicitly unstable interface</name>
  <decision>Publish, in both the operator `--output` flag help and the docs-site CLI guide, that `--output text` is a human-readable view and NOT a stable interface, and that `--output json` is the interface to script against.</decision>
  <context>
06-CONTEXT.md D-03 locks this and rates it **one-way**. Once the CLI has told operators the text lane
is unstable and subsequent phases evolve it (Phase 7 adds six record-state fields to these same
reports), the prior sentence-shaped output cannot be restored as a contract — any consumer that
depended on it has already been told not to. The reverse direction, re-declaring text stable, would
require freezing a lane that by then has changed shape across every report. Nothing in Task 2 or
Task 3 runs until this is confirmed.
  </context>
  <read_first>
    .planning/phases/06-typed-operator-renderer/06-CONTEXT.md §Text Lane Status (D-03) and §Testing (D-08),
    docs-site/src/content/docs/guides/cli.md lines 105-135 (the wording being replaced)
  </read_first>
  <options>
    <option id="publish-unstable">
      <name>Publish as unstable — the locked D-03 decision</name>
      <pros>Text may change freely, forever; no goldens, no exact-output gate, no maintenance surface on the lane. This is what makes D-08's structural-only pinning honest rather than a gap, and it is what lets Phase 7 add six fields to every report without a compatibility argument.</pros>
      <cons>One-way: a consumer that had been parsing the text lane loses any claim to stability, and the guarantee cannot be reinstated later.</cons>
    </option>
    <option id="stay-silent">
      <name>Say nothing and leave stability undefined</name>
      <pros>Changes no user-visible string in this phase.</pros>
      <cons>Leaves a caller to discover the guarantee by breakage, contradicting the correct-by-reading principle (durable record `4aksmneehh`), and invites someone to parse the view — which is precisely why YAML and TOON were rejected as the text format in the first place. Rejected during discussion.</cons>
    </option>
