## Flagged assumptions (spec-less probe)

- A VACUOUS gate (no verdict at or above the threshold) fails rather than passes: D-03's gate "validates the threshold", which zero confident verdicts cannot do. This is the non-vacuity of the existing gate, not a new gate.
- Bucket edges are the spike's (0.70, 0.90); the gate row uses the resolved threshold separately, so a non-default threshold still gets its own gate row.
