<objective>
Add the store-layer supersession primitive: two optional `*string` link fields on `Memory`
(`supersedes` / `superseded_by`) with their payload codec, the `Store.Supersede` method that
owner-gates the target and back-stamps it vector-preservingly, the `store.ErrAlreadySuperseded`
single-hop guard, and the recall-gate soft-hide that excludes superseded records from
`search_memory` / `list_memory` while leaving `get_memory` fetchable.

Purpose: This is the correctable-recall core of the milestone — a wrong/stale memory can be
superseded by a correcting one, history is preserved (nothing deleted or overwritten), and the
superseded record disappears from recall but stays retrievable by id. Every piece composes an
existing, tested in-repo primitive (`SetVisibility` for the gated mutation, `activeWindowConditions`
for the recall gate, `getWritable` for authz, `NotBefore`/`NotAfter` for the optional-field codec).

Output: `internal/store/store.go` (fields, codec, method, sentinel, recall-gate additions) and
`internal/store/store_test.go` (six `TestSupersede*` cases) — all green under `task test`.
</objective>
