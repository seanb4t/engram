<objective>
Normalize every escaped key-link `pattern:` field in this repo to D-02's escape-free character-class
form, then turn the guard on as a recurring gate with D-04's two different scopes.

Purpose: 39 pattern fields across 20 plan files currently reach the consuming tool's regex engine
meaning "a literal backslash, then any character" — valid regex, silently unmatchable. Normalizing
them without also landing the gate leaves the repo one plan away from the same state; landing the
gate without normalizing first leaves the build permanently red. Both happen here, in that order.
Output: 20 rewritten plan files in one commit, and `internal/keylinks/gate_test.go`.
</objective>
