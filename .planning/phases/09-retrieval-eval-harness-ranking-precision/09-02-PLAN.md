D-01/D-02). The score is already shipped end-to-end — `store.Memory.Score` (from

closure is typed `(*mcp.CallToolResult, any, error)` like all 13 tools, so the
go-sdk omits any auto-generated output schema; the durable client-visible channel
is the tool's `Description` prose plus the docs-site.

Purpose: make the near-miss score legible to callers/agents and honest to #261
traceability (D-04).
