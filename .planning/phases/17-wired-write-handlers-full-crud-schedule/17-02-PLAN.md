   populate their proto responses without re-fetching (review HIGH).

   methods + storeRule/listRules, `updateArgs.Content` → `*string` with vector-vs-
   payload routing, typed rule/stale-summary sentinels, and the proto tags-only
   doc reconciliation.

17-04. This plan makes those buildable.
