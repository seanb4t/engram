<objective>
Ship man pages for the released binary. A new hidden `engram man <dir>` command (`cmd/engram/man.go`)
wraps `cobra/doc.GenManTree` over the live `rootCmd` with a pinned, release-independent header — Date
= Unix epoch in UTC (D-01), Source = `engram <version>` from the raw ldflags `version` var (D-02),
Manual = `Engram Manual` — and `rootCmd.DisableAutoGenTag = true` drops the date-bearing `HISTORY`
footer tree-wide (D-03), so re-running the same binary is byte-identical (D-04). The page set is
cobra's own unfiltered `IsAvailableCommand()` walk: the visible `--help` tree plus the `completion`
subtree, automatically excluding the hidden `man` command itself, `help`, and the two
`Deprecated`-marked aliases (D-05, D-06). The Homebrew cask's hand-rolled `post_install` /
`post_uninstall` hook pair in `.goreleaser.yaml` grows a fourth, symmetric man-page step after the
completions step (D-07, D-08, D-09), and `cmd/engram/releaseconfig_test.go` pins its ordering, counts,
and absences the way it already does for completions. Zero new Go dependencies — `cobra` is already a
direct requirement and `cobra/doc` is a subpackage of it; `go.mod`/`go.sum` do not change at all
(RESEARCH.md's verified zero-line `go mod tidy` diff corrects the milestone's "promote to direct"
wording — there is nothing to promote).

Purpose: shell completions shipped in `2026-08-23.01` Phase 1 (PR #515); man pages are the one
remaining "shipped alongside the binary" surface, and the live-binary hook shape makes them a
fail-closed proof that the installed binary works (REQ-manpages-generated,
REQ-manpages-cask-installed).

Output: `cmd/engram/man.go` (new), `cmd/engram/man_test.go` (new), an extended
`TestReleaseConfigCaskInstallGate`, and the two hook-body extensions in `.goreleaser.yaml`. Independent
of plan 01-01; no shared files.
