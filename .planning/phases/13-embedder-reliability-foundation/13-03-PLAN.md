<objective>
Complete SC3 by stamping the embedder-config-identity onto records rewritten by `engram reindex` — the FIFTH document-embed write site, which the other four (13-02) do not cover because it uses a fundamentally different mechanism (REQ-embed-config-identity / DECISION 3, D-05).

This is the plan the phase research flagged as carrying the two landmines; it is deliberately isolated so neither is glossed:
1. `Store.Reindex` upserts the source payload VERBATIM (`Payload: p.Payload`) and never round-trips through `Memory`/`payload()` — a load-bearing invariant that keeps a pre-isolation record's `owner` key ABSENT. The stamp here must be a direct raw-map key write, NOT the `Memory.EmbedderIdentity` field path used by the other four sites (Research Pitfall 1).
2. `StoreAndEmbedderFromEnvNoEnsure` loads `*config.Config` internally then DISCARDS it, so reindex has no path to the identity string today. Its signature must grow a return value, rippling to three call sites (Research Pitfall 2).

A third hazard surfaced in cross-AI review (Codex MEDIUM): the resume-skip path can skip BEFORE the stamp is written. The resume skip predicate at `internal/store/store.go:2125` compares target CONTENT only, and `reindexTargetContents` (store.go:2165) returns only `id → content`. Under `Resume:true`, a target whose content already matches but which lacks (or has a mismatched) `embedder_identity` would hit `res.Unchanged++; continue` and be left UNSTAMPED — silently undercutting SC3 (every record traceable to its embedder config). Task 1 therefore makes the resume predicate identity-aware.

Purpose: reindexed records are traceable to the embedder config that produced them, closing the last gap in the per-record provenance stamp — including on resume.
Output: `ReindexOptions.Identity`, the guarded raw-map stamp in `Reindex`, an identity-aware resume skip predicate (`reindexTargetContents` extended to carry the target identity), the amended verbatim-payload doc comment, the extended `StoreAndEmbedderFromEnvNoEnsure` signature with its three updated callers, and reindex-stamps-identity integration tests (including a resume-restamp case).
</objective>
