<objective>
Prove ROADMAP success criterion 3 — `schema_version` is a plain wire-visible field on `store.Memory`
(never `json:"-"`), observable on `full=true` recall and `get_memory` — and that D-11's scoping
holds: the compact `recallView` stays untouched.

Purpose: this is the deliberate divergence from the twice-established `json:"-"` precedent
(`EmbedderIdentity`, `IdempotencyFingerprint`), both of whose comments call the hidden tag
"deliberate and load-bearing". A future reader pattern-matching those two neighbours would "fix" the
new field into `json:"-"` and quietly close the wire. The assertions here are what stop that, and
they run in `internal/server` — the package that actually serves the wire — rather than only in the
package that declares the struct.

Output: a wire-shape test mirroring the existing embedder-identity guards, in the opposite
direction.
</objective>
