## Naming decision (Claude's discretion, resolved)

The client-tier verb is **`engram migration-status`**, not `engram status`.

- The operator-tier sibling is `engram migrate status`; a client-tier `migrate status` is not
  available, because tier classification is by the presence of the `server` flag and the two would
  collide on one command key.
- `engram status` reads as "server status" and this command reports exactly one thing — a
  schema-version histogram. The CLI is correct-by-reading (standing invariant `4aksmneehh`), so the
  verb names what it reports and leaves `engram status` free for a future health verb.
- Hyphenated multi-word verbs are house style: `migrate-remap-owner`, `prune-expired`,
  `summarize-missing`, `backfill-short-ids`.
