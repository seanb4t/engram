<objective>
Build the exhaustive field-mapping proof that `REQ-connect-parity-roundtrip-proof` demands: one
shared reflection detector that derives its inclusion rule from `json:"-"` (D-05), a permanent
negative fixture that proves that detector can reject (D-07), a reflection auto-filled fixture
that proves every mapped proto field is POPULATED rather than merely present (D-06), and an inline
decode-back comparison (D-08) — with no production inverse mapper and no hand-maintained list.

Purpose: `buf breaking` operates on the `.proto` schema and has zero visibility into
`memoryToProto`'s Go body. It is structurally incapable of catching the gap that recurred across
v0.8.x, v0.11.x and v0.13.x. This test is the only mechanism that inspects the actual mapping.

Output: `internal/server/connectapi_parity_test.go`, containing a detector whose range is proven
non-trivial on every CI run.
</objective>
