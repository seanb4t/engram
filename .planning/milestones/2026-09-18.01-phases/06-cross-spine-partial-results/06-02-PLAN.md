publish the third state everywhere the first two are already documented.

form slots into it and both call sites gain one argument that Go forces them to pass.

third state is documented as distinct from truncation), D-01 (the upgrade guide tells a caller that
a call which used to fail now succeeds).

surfaces telling the same story as the wire.
