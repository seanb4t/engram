<objective>
Build the ordered half of the shared bounded-read mechanism and record the call-site inventory.
`scrollOrderedPage` generalizes `listByCursor`'s keyset paging from one RPC per page to several
small RPCs per logical page: each RPC is sized from plan 03-02's per-view ceiling so it cannot
overflow, the page accumulates the measured bytes of what it received and stops at the page byte
budget or the record limit, and the resume position is the existing `listCursor` shape. The
inventory (D-08) assigns every Qdrant read site in `internal/store` to the phase that migrates it
or to a written exemption, and is what Phase 5's closing check compares against.

Decisions implemented: D-02 (small RPCs + a running measured-byte total; count from the ceiling),
D-03 item 1 (an ordered-page helper in `internal/store`, generalizing `listByCursor`'s
`created_at` `start_from` + boundary-id idiom, classified in the recall gate in the same change),
D-04 (takes the caller's `readView`), D-05 (the two-phase ids→payload design is not adopted, so its
TOCTOU and `GetPoints`-order criteria do not apply — stated as a must-have so the verifier does not
look for them), D-06 (the 2 MiB budgets from plan 03-02), D-07 (batch-of-1 fallback, then the named
error), D-08 (the inventory). Framing (D-00): the keyset mechanism Qdrant's own documentation
prescribes for a non-unique `order_by` key — `start_from` plus a `must_not` `has_id` filter of ids
already returned — rather than `listByCursor`'s client-side over-fetch, because an over-fetch of
`len(seen)` extra records can exceed the byte-derived per-RPC count (at 2 records per RPC a tie of
3 could never advance). Discretion exercised: the helper exposes budget truncation explicitly
(`CutByBudget`, `Exhausted`, `Next`) — RESEARCH's return shape 1 — because D-02 requires the page to
stop on bytes, which makes a short, non-final page unavoidable and therefore something Phase 4 must
be able to see.

Not in scope: wiring `Store.List`, `listByCursor` or `ListScheduled` onto the helper (Phase 4, which
also moves the helper to `recallTransmitters` in that change), and tie-safety for more than
`maxListLimit` records at one timestamp under concurrent inserts (REQ-cursor-tie-safety, v2).

Purpose: REQ-byte-budget-pages (ordered primitive, accumulated page budget) and the inventory half of
REQ-bounded-read-mechanism (Phase 5 closes it).

Output: `internal/store/orderedpage.go`, its recall-gate classification, real-Qdrant proofs, and
`03-INVENTORY.md`.
</objective>
