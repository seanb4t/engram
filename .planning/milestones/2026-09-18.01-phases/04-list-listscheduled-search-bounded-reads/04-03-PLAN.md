D-05 (the assembly loop and its explicit revision of Phase 3 D-06, now written into
`boundedread.go`'s own doc comment).

Purpose: REQ-list-bounded's deep-offset mode and REQ-list-scheduled-bounded.

Output: a keys-only read view, a prefix walk, a bounded `ListScheduled`, and two real-Qdrant
regressions.
