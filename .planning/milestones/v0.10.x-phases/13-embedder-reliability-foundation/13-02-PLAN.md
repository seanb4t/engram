<objective>
Deliver the embedder-config-identity stamp on the four write paths that go through `Memory`/`payload()` (`store_memory`, `schedule_memory`, `update_memory`, `store_discovery`) plus `store_rule` (REQ-embed-config-identity / DECISION 3, D-01..D-06). Every newly stored document-embed record carries a `v1:`-prefixed short hash of the fields that determine its stored document vector, so a future reindex-boundary audit (deferred) can detect mixed-embedding-space records.

This plan covers the FOUR clean write sites and the shared store codec. The fifth site — `Store.Reindex` — uses a divergent verbatim-payload mechanism and a `StoreAndEmbedderFromEnvNoEnsure` signature change, planned separately in `13-03` (it depends on the helper and payload-key const defined here).

Purpose: per-record embedding-space provenance without touching recall semantics, the reranker, or the proto wire.
Output: the pure `config.EmbedderIdentity` helper, `Memory.EmbedderIdentity` (tagged `json:"-"`, persisted only through the manual payload codec) + its payload round-trip under a shared key const, `deps.embedderIdentity` wiring, five stamped write sites, per-site positive persistence tests, and the D-06 negative-space guards at all three full-response wire paths.
</objective>
