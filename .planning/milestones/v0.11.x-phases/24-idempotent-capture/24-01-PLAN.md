<objective>
Land the payload-only idempotency primitives with zero behavior change to any live path:
the content-fingerprint payload field on `Memory`, the distinct `ErrIdempotencyConflict`
sentinel, and the two pure functions that make idempotency work — `idempotencyPointID`
(deterministic UUIDv5 over `(owner, scope, key)`) and `contentFingerprint` (sha256 over the
client-authored fields). Nothing calls the helpers yet; this plan proves they are correct in
isolation so the Wave-2 handler wiring is a pure composition step.

Purpose: Separate the pure, Qdrant-free, deterministically-testable core (derivation + fingerprint
+ codec) from the integration-tier handler wiring (Plan 02), so the derivation's injectivity and
the fingerprint's stability are pinned before any concurrency or Qdrant behavior is exercised.

Output: `Memory.IdempotencyFingerprint` + codec + sentinel (internal/store); `engramIdempotencyNS`,
`idempotencyPointID`, `contentFingerprint` (internal/server/idempotency.go); pure-function unit
tests for all of the above.
</objective>
