<objective>
Build the OIDC client-credentials service lane inside `internal/auth`, and prove — as the FIRST
test of the phase — that an authenticated service principal whose configured owner claim cannot be
resolved is REJECTED at the verifier boundary (fail-closed) rather than silently mapped to the
anonymous empty-owner bucket. This closes the milestone's #1 risk (SC2 / D-08 / D-09 / D-10).

The service lane reuses the existing `ClaimIdentity` + `namespacedOwner` resolution verbatim with
its own owner-claim order (`["client_id","azp"]`, D-05) and its own independently-configurable
audience (D-14), and adds exactly one new behavior over the human lane: a fail-closed reject on an
empty resolved owner.

Purpose: eliminate the anonymous-bucket bypass at the earliest possible layer (a clean 401 from
`mcpauth.RequireBearerToken`), not late inside a tool handler.
Output: `Verifier.failClosed` field + reject branch + `NewService` per-lane constructor in
`auth.go`; the FIRST fail-closed regression test.
</objective>
