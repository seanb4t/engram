<objective>
Prove the Jev reranker end to end on one thin path, then pin the store composition's edges.

- **Tracer (Task 1):** a server-held rank hook (`relevance.Hook` over a real `jev` client against an httptest Decisions endpoint) flows through `deps.searchMemory` into `store.SearchReranked`, which fetches the CandidateK pool, applies the unchanged lexical step to the whole pool, sends ONE Decisions request with one noul question per candidate, stable-sorts by P(relevant), truncates to k, and stamps `Memory.Relevance`; Connect `SearchMemories` carries it on a new additive `optional double relevance = 31;` proto field. Failure falls back to today's order.
- **Store edges (Task 2, TDD):** byte-identical nil-hook path, ties, fallback matrix, promotion beyond k, foreign ids, empty pool, no input mutation, authz-scoped hook input, pool size at the recall maximum.

Decisions implemented: D-03 (lexical first, stable sort by Jev, any failure keeps lexical order), D-04 (whole pool, one request, truncate after), D-05 (per-hit `relevance`, omitempty; the Connect proto field here, MCP compact and CLI in plan 04-04), D-08 (verdict.State reuse at 600 code points with a total-state guard; the guard's edges are pinned in plan 04-02), D-07 (the SearchReranked lanes — MCP and Connect `search_memory`; `search_discovery` is plan 04-05).

Config-driven construction of the hook (`ENGRAM_SEARCH_RANKER`, D-01) and the dedicated no-retry client (D-09) are plan 04-03's expansion; this tracer injects the hook into `deps` directly so the architecture (store layering, decision wire, proto field) is proven first.

Purpose: surface a dead end in the store/decide layering, the decision wire shape or the proto field after one commit.
Output: `internal/relevance`, `store.RankHook`/`RankWithHook`/`Memory.Relevance`, `deps.rankHook`, the proto field and regenerated code, tests.
