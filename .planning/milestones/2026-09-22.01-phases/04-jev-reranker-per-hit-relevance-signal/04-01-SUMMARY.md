**A server-held Jev rank hook now reorders `search_memory`'s Connect lane by per-hit P(relevant), stamping an additive `relevance` proto field, with the whole candidate pool sent in one Decisions request and any failure degrading silently to today's lexical order.**

## Accomplishments

- New `internal/relevance` package: the spike-004 noul question set, a D-08 budgeted request builder (`NewRequest`) with a deterministic per-candidate shrink loop, response mapping (`FromResponse`), and `Hook` — a `store.RankHook` factory over any `decide.Decider` (nil-safe).
- `internal/store/rerank.go` gained `RankHook`, `applyRelevance` (full-coverage + finite [0,1] validation, stable-sort by relevance descending, ties keep lexical order), `applyRankHook` (never calls the hook on an empty pool), and `RankWithHook` (nil hook = today's identical `rankCandidates` call; non-nil hook ranks the WHOLE pool before truncating to k, per D-04). `rankCandidates`'s body is untouched.
- `store.Memory.Relevance *float64` (transient, like `Score`) and `store.SearchOptions.RankHook` wired through `SearchReranked`, which now composes `RankWithHook(ctx, query, hits, k, opts.RankHook)` as its final step.
- `engram.v1.Memory.relevance` (`optional double`, field 31) — additive, `buf breaking` clean against `main`; `gen/go`, `gen/ts`, `ui/src/lib/gen`, and the vendored console bundle (`internal/webauth/static`, drifted by the proto comment reaching generated TS doc comments) regenerated and committed.
- `deps.rankHook` threaded into `searchMemory`'s `SearchOptions`; `memoryToProto` copies (never aliases) `Memory.Relevance` onto the wire.
- `TestSearchRerankJevTracer`: a real `jev.Client` against an `httptest` Decisions endpoint proves one search sends exactly one request carrying all 5 candidates (never just k=2), the provider's highest-scored record (which the lexical step ranked dead last, zero overlap) comes back first with `Relevance` 0.97, a 503 falls back to the identical lexical-order IDs with every `Relevance` nil, and a nil hook makes zero Decisions calls.
- Task 2: 11 new store-layer tests pin the byte-identical nil-hook default, ties-keep-lexical-order, promotion beyond k, the full D-03 fallback matrix (hook error, nil map, missing hit, NaN/±Inf, out-of-[0,1]), the `[0,1]` boundary values themselves (0 and 1 are accepted, not rejected), foreign-id rejection, the empty-pool hook skip, no-input-mutation, and — against a real Qdrant — that the hook sees exactly the authz-filtered pool (two owners, one shared record) and never more than `CandidateK`'s bound (32 at k=2, 100 at k=1000, against 105 seeded records).
