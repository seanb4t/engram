<objective>
Author the independent paraphrase case's inputs (D-01, D-02, D-03, D-12):

- A new 96-record, multi-domain, synthetic corpus with deliberate sticky neighbours.
- 20 single-answer topic labels plus 4 no-answer labels.
- A hermetic integrity test that enforces every D-02 and D-03 property mechanically.
- The labels-only prompt, and a **blocking checkpoint** at which a fresh context writes the queries without ever seeing the corpus.

Why a checkpoint: the executor that writes the corpus has, by construction, seen every target. It cannot also be the blind author D-01 requires. So the query-writing step is handed to a context that has seen nothing, either a newly dispatched subagent or the user. That is the one thing this plan cannot automate. Plan 01-04 then maps each query to its target by topic ID (D-01's "separate pass") and records the procedure in the fixture comment.

Purpose: RANK-01's paraphrase case must be written independently of #261's targets, so that RANK-02's keep/demote/replace decision rests on queries nobody tuned. The spike fixture was target-aware, so this fixes that flaw.
Output: `paraphrase_fixture.go` and its test, `01-BLIND-QUERY-PROMPT.md`, and `01-BLIND-QUERIES.md`.
</objective>
