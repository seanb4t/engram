  <decision>
The shape of the hidden count is a published contract on two Connect responses, two MCP results and the CLI JSON output. Removing or renaming it later is a breaking change on all three lanes (D-01 records it as one-way). D-01 locked THAT it ships and that it splits per state; the exact shape was left to the planner. One correctness problem decides between the shapes: a record can carry several states at once (archived and superseded, for example). If the per-state counts are summed to produce the header's "N hidden by recall gate", a record with two states is counted twice and the header over-reports — the entry point would lie.
  </decision>
