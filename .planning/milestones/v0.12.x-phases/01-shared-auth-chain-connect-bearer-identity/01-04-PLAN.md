    anonymous empty-owner bucket. Name the two ways to configure a lane.
  - A bearer-authenticated Connect caller is exempt from the `X-CSRF-Token` double-submit check that
    cookie-authenticated browser callers must satisfy; the exemption is decided by which lane
    verified the request, never by which headers the caller sent.
